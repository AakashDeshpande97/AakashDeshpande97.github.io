BAJA SAE India: off-road vehicle chassis

TypeStudent team
When2017 and 2018
ContextHead of chassis department

Overview

BAJA SAE India is a competition to design and build an all-terrain vehicle within a strict rule book. I led the chassis department and was one of the five members who represented the team at the virtual BAJA SAE India event.

FOS 1.48in the front-impact simulation
376 MPamaximum combined stress, 7.65 mm deformation
5 load casesfront, side, rear impact, roll-over and bump

How it works

  1. Fix the layoutAgreed wheelbase and width with the steering team.
  2. Model the frameBuilt the roll hoops, side-impact members, bracings and more in SolidWorks.
  3. Choose sectionsPrimary and secondary circular tubes in AISI 4130 or 1018 steel.
  4. SimulateMeshed the frame and ran static analyses for every load case in ANSYS.
  5. Check safetyVon Mises stress, deformation and factor of safety before fabrication.

What I did

  • Designed the frame to the rule book's limits on dimensions and safety, while keeping driver ergonomics in mind.
  • Kept weight down without giving up strength by using two tube cross-sections, primary and secondary, and choosing materials by yield strength and strength-to-weight ratio.
  • For each impact case, picked a realistic G-force, applied it to the frame (for example 10 G at the front with the rear fixed) and judged the design by stress, displacement and factor of safety.
