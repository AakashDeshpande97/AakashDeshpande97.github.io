SUPRA SAE India: Formula student race car steering

TypeStudent team
When2017 and 2018
ContextSteering department

Overview

SUPRA SAE India is a Formula student competition to design and build a Formula 3-style car. I worked in the steering department and designed the vehicle's steering system.

Rack & pinionchosen for stability, control and low vibration
Ackermanngeometry built and iterated in AutoCAD and CATIA

How it works

  1. Set the basicsWheelbase, track width and wheel size.
  2. Agree on armsSteering arm angle and length with the suspension team.
  3. Draw the geometryAckermann geometry in AutoCAD or CATIA.
  4. IterateRack travel, stops and tie-rod lengths, by trial and error.
  5. VerifyTurning radius and Ackermann percentage, to judge over- or understeer.

What I did

  • Calculated the turning radius and the maximum and minimum wheel angles for full left and right turns, and decided where the rack sits on the chassis.
  • Iterated the geometry until the rack displacement, stops and tie-rod lengths were fixed, then read the resulting turning radius from the drawing.
  • Checked the Ackermann percentage: above 100% means oversteer, below means understeer. 100% is the ideal, rarely reached in practice.
