Real-time object detection on a Raspberry Pi for an autonomous robot

TypeTeam project
WhenMay – Jul 2025
ContextUniversity of Siegen
View code on GitHub

Overview

A Fischertechnik robot with a webcam and a Raspberry Pi 5 that detects a cat and follows it. The detector is a fine-tuned YOLOv8n running through NCNN on the Pi's ARM CPU, and its output drives two motion controllers.

8–9 FPSreal-time inference on an ARM CPU, no GPU
110 msper frame with NCNN, against 350 ms in PyTorch
17,588images in the training dataset

How it works

  1. Build the dataset17,588 images from COCO, Roboflow and our own recordings, balanced cat vs. non-cat.
  2. Choose a modelCompared YOLOv5, YOLOv8n, Faster R-CNN, EfficientDet and RetinaNet.
  3. Fine-tuneFroze backbone and neck, trained the detection head in two stages.
  4. OptimiseBenchmarked TorchScript, PyTorch, ONNX and NCNN on the Pi.
  5. ControlA PD controller centres the cat; a P controller keeps the distance.

What I did

  • Built and balanced the dataset with augmentation (flips, rotations, brightness, HSV) and YOLO-assisted labelling.
  • Fine-tuned YOLOv8n by freezing the first 21 layers and training the head for cat vs. non-cat, in two stages of 30 and 20 epochs.
  • Benchmarked export formats on the Raspberry Pi 5 and picked NCNN for real-time speed.
  • Turned detections directly into motion: the horizontal offset of the box drives the steering, the box area drives the speed.
