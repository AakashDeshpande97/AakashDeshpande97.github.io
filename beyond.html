Away from the keyboard

Railfanning

Trains have fascinated me for as long as I can remember. It all began with spotting trains in India, then learning everything about them: the technical details, the power of the engines, the different versions and their history, and visiting railway museums. I've carried it on in Germany. At the DB Museum in Nuremberg I saw the Adler, the first locomotive to run in Germany.

Cricket

Cricket clears my mind and restores my energy, and it's one of the things that keeps me excited about life. Even in Germany, where it's hardly played, I stay in touch: I follow Royal Challengers Bengaluru closely and feel deeply attached to the team, especially after they won their first IPL title after 18 years of trying.

Sketching

I've always had a knack for art, and I still enjoy sketching with pencil and paper. It started long before I learned CAD.
